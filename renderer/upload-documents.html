Matching guidance and supported documents

Matching guidance
Folders can use the surname alone GONZAGA or surname first followed by the first name, such as GONZAGA ROMEL. When surnames repeat, the full first name is preferred; an initial can still narrow a match when initials differ. A date or day number (1 to 31) can also match the CF2 treatment dates. Examples: GONZAGA 15, GONZAGA AUG 13-15, or folders.

Beabots supported documents to upload
SOA - SOA (GONZAGA SOA), CSF - CSF (GONZAGA CSF), PBEF - COE (GONZAGA PBEF), CF4 - OTH (GONZAGA CF4), AF - AF (GONZAGA AF), TX - OTH (GONZAGA TX)
