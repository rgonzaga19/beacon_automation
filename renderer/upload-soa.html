SOA File Naming Guide

  1. First, create the patient SOA in SOA EXCEL GENERATOR
  2. Name the file with the surname, GONZAGA or followed by the patient's first name: GONZAGA ROMEL
  3. A surname-only filename is accepted when it identifies one patient: GONZAGA
  4. For two-word surnames, keep one space between the surname words: DEL ROSARIO JUAN
  5. For matching surnames, an initial can distinguish patients when the initials differ: ABAO A or ABAO B
  6. If patients share both surname and first initial, use the full first name: ABAO ARWIN and ABAO ALLEN
  7. Add a treatment date or day to help distinguish matching surnames: ABAO AUG 13-15 or ABAO 15
  8. Do not include suffixes such as Jr., Sr., II, or III
  9. Locate and upload the files here. then input the transmittal numbers
  10. The accuracy of your upload relies on the SOA Excel filename. Beabots use this filename to match each uploaded file with its corresponding transmittal number (derived from Beacon CF2 data).
