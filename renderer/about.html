BEABOTS · BEACON CLAIMS OPERATIONS

Claims work, organized from preparation through review.

Beabots is a web workspace for Nephro Group Dialysis Centers teams working with claims in Beacon. It brings claim preparation, document handling, Beacon workflow automation, and follow-up tools together so staff can move through repetitive steps in one place.

ONE WORKSPACE, CONNECTED STEPS

From source files to claim follow-up

Choose a focused tool for a single task or use the full workflow to connect several claim steps.

01

Prepare claim data

Build and upload workbooks for new or existing CF2 drafts. Generate SOA spreadsheets individually or in batches, then prepare CF4 details from claim information.

CF2 drafts · SOA workbooks · CF4
02

Run Beacon workflows

Automate steps across a claim, including draft creation, matching SOA uploads, eSOA generation, and CF4 encoding. Process completed workbook rows sequentially.

Full workflow · Automation runs
03

Manage documents

Match patient folders to transmittals for supported document uploads. Group PDF files, review proposed filenames, and download organized output.

Document upload · PDF rename
04

Review and follow up

Find Beacon claims by transmittal, patient, or admission date. Review and edit draft claim details, run batch actions, and coordinate with your team in chat.

Claim lookup · Editing · Team chat

Security, privacy, and output agreement.

Please review these responsibilities before using the workspace.

01 / RESPONSIBLE ACCESS

Security and account use

Keep your password and Beacon credentials confidential, use an appropriately secured device, and sign out when finished. Access should be limited to people authorized by your organization. Do not attempt to bypass access controls or use the workspace for another person's information without proper authority.

Saved Beacon credentials are encrypted before storage. No online service can promise that every system, network, or device is free from security incidents. You remain responsible for following your organization's security procedures and reporting suspected unauthorized access promptly.

02 / PERSONAL INFORMATION

Privacy and sensitive data

Claim workflows can contain personal and sensitive health information. Upload or enter only information you are authorized to handle and only when needed for your work. Use the information for its intended claims purpose, follow your organization's retention and access rules, and avoid sharing files or account access with unauthorized people.

Information submitted to Beacon may also be subject to Beacon's systems, terms, and privacy practices. Review the applicable policies of your organization and the services you use. Contact the Beabots administrator through the About page for questions about this workspace's data handling.

03 / HUMAN REVIEW IS REQUIRED

Accuracy, judgment, and transmission

Beabots output is generated from information supplied by users and automated processing. It may contain omissions, mismatches, formatting problems, or other errors. Results are aids to workflow and are not a substitute for professional judgment, official requirements, or review of source records.

An authorized, qualified person must independently verify every relevant field, attachment, patient match, date, code, and supporting document against authoritative records and current requirements before transmission. After transmission, the responsible person must review the submitted claim and any Beacon confirmations, notices, rejections, or requests for correction, and take any necessary follow-up action.

Do not transmit a claim solely because Beabots produced or displayed an output. The user and their organization remain responsible for the correctness, completeness, authorization, and submission of all data and documents.
