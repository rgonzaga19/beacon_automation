01 / RESPONSIBLE ACCESS

Security and account use

Keep your password and Beacon credentials confidential, use an appropriately secured device, and sign out when finished. Access should be limited to people authorized by your organization. Do not attempt to bypass access controls or use the workspace for another person's information without proper authority.

Saved Beacon credentials are encrypted before storage. No online service can promise that every system, network, or device is free from security incidents. You remain responsible for following your organization's security procedures and reporting suspected unauthorized access promptly.

02 / PERSONAL INFORMATION

Privacy and sensitive data

Claim workflows can contain personal and sensitive health information. Upload or enter only information you are authorized to handle and only when needed for your work. Use the information for its intended claims purpose, follow your organization's retention and access rules, and avoid sharing files or account access with unauthorized people.

Information submitted to Beacon may also be subject to Beacon's systems, terms, and privacy practices. Review the applicable policies of your organization and the services you use. Contact the Beabots administrator through the About page for questions about this workspace's data handling.
